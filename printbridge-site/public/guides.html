Zebra printers & Mac guides

Setup steps and specs for each Zebra model CrispDock supports, and fixes for the problems Mac users run into most.

CrispDock is a macOS printer driver for Zebra ZPL label printers. It works from any app’s Print dialog on macOS 13 or later, on Apple silicon and Intel. CrispDock was tested on real ZP 500, ZP 505 and GK420d printers; the other models use their published specs.

Printers

  • Zebra ZD421 driver for MacA common 4-inch shipping printer; 203 or 300 dpi.
  • Zebra ZD420 driver for MacThe ZD421’s predecessor, still in many warehouses.
  • Zebra ZD411 driver for MacCompact 2-inch printer (also covers the ZD410).
  • Zebra ZD220 / ZD230 driver for MacZebra’s value 4-inch desktop printers.
  • Zebra ZD620 / ZD621 driver for MacZebra’s fastest 4-inch desktop printers.
  • Zebra ZP 450 driver for MacOlder 4-inch shipping printer, still widely used.
  • Zebra ZP 500 / ZP 505 driver for MacTested on real hardware.
  • Zebra GK420d driver for MacTested on real hardware (GK420t supported too).
  • Zebra GX420d driver for MacFaster sibling of the GK420 (GX420t and GX430t too).
  • Zebra GC420d driver for MacEntry-level 4-inch desktop printer (GC420t too).
  • Zebra LP 2844 driver for MacLP 2844-Z (ZPL) supported; EPL-only LP 2844 isn’t.
  • Zebra ZT series driver for MacIndustrial printers, ZT111 to ZT620.

Not supported: EPL-only printers (such as the original LP 2844), CPCL mobile printers and Bluetooth connections.

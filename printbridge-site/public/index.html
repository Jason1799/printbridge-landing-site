Everything the built-in driver gets wrong

macOS ships a generic Zebra driver that blurs barcodes and streams labels slowly. CrispDock converts every page into clean printer commands made for thermal labels.

Sharp barcodes

Bars, 2D codes and small print land on the printer's dot grid, so scanners read them first time.

No stop-and-go

Each label is compressed and sent in one piece, so the printer runs smoothly instead of pausing mid-label.

Any app, same size

Print from Chrome, Safari, Preview or any PDF app with ⌘P. Margins added by the app are ignored — the label always fills 4×6.

Labels on letter sheets

Labels printed for 8.5×11 paper — one or two per page, framed or not, sideways or upright — are found, cut out and rotated automatically.

USB or network

The installer finds Zebra printers on USB and your network, and detects 203, 300 or 600 dpi heads.

Preview before you print

Drop a PDF, PNG, JPEG or iPhone photo into the CrispDock app and see every label exactly as the printer will get it.

See every label before it prints

The CrispDock app shows the exact output of the driver — one tile per label. A sheet with two shipping labels becomes two upright 4×6 labels.

Works with

Zebra printers that speak ZPL — for example GK420, GX420/430, ZD410/420/421/620, ZP 450/500/505 (ZPL) and ZT series — over USB, Ethernet or Wi-Fi, at 203, 300 or 600 dpi.

Not supported

EPL-only models such as the LP 2844, CPCL mobile printers, and Bluetooth connections (macOS can't print to them).

Questions

Do I need Zebra's own driver?

No. CrispDock is the driver. Zebra doesn't publish one for macOS, and the generic one built into macOS is what causes fuzzy, slow labels.

Which apps and websites can I print from?

Anything that prints on a Mac: browsers, Preview, PDF apps. Labels from marketplaces, shipping tools and carrier sites all work, whether they come as 4×6 pages or on letter-size sheets.

What does the free version leave out?

Nothing — every feature works. Each label just prints with a “CrispDock FREE” watermark repeated across the whole label. Any paid plan removes it.

How does the licence work?

Right after payment you get a licence key by email. Paste it into the CrispDock app to activate. Each key works on one Mac; deactivate it in the app (or your account) to move the key to a new Mac.

Which version of CrispDock do I need?

Pro keys need CrispDock 1.3 or later — older versions can't activate them. Download the latest version from this page and install it over the old one; your printers and settings stay.

How does the 7-day free trial work?

The yearly plan starts with 7 days free for new customers, with no watermark. You enter a card at checkout, but nothing is charged until the trial ends — the date is in your licence email and on your account page. Cancel from your account before then and you pay nothing; otherwise the yearly price is charged and the plan renews each year. Lifetime has no trial, but the free version lets you try everything with the watermark first.

How do I cancel a subscription?

Use “Manage subscription” on this page to sign in with your email, then cancel. Nothing is charged after that, and Pro keeps working until the end of the period you've already paid for. The same page lets you change your card and download invoices.

What happens when a subscription ends?

When the paid period ends — because you cancelled, or a renewal payment failed and the card wasn't updated within 24 hours — Pro keeps working for 24 more hours. After that, labels print with the free watermark again. Nothing else changes: your key, Macs and settings stay, and renewing turns Pro back on with the same key.

Can I switch from a subscription to Lifetime?

Yes, from your account page — you keep the same key and Macs. The unused days left in your current billing year are taken off the Lifetime price ($79, or $119 with AirShare) and you pay the difference. Time you've already used isn't credited, and the subscription itself isn't refunded. For example, switching from yearly Pro to Pro Lifetime with half the year left costs about $64. During the free trial nothing has been paid yet, so there's nothing to credit.

What is AirShare?

CrispDock can share this Mac's Zebra printers on your Wi‑Fi, so iPhones, iPads and other Macs can print to them. With Pro, labels printed that way carry the watermark; Pro + AirShare removes it. Labels you print on the Mac itself have no watermark on either plan. Already on Pro? Add AirShare from your account: subscriptions pay only the difference for the rest of the period, and Pro Lifetime upgrades for $40.

What is your refund policy?

If CrispDock isn't right for you, contact us within 30 days of a payment and we'll refund that payment in full. Once a payment is refunded, its licence key stops working right away.

Are my labels uploaded anywhere?

No. Every label is converted on your Mac. CrispDock only goes online to activate your licence and to check it now and then — printing works offline. A subscription is renewed in the background whenever the Mac is online; a Mac that stays offline past the end of a paid period plus 24 hours prints with the watermark until it reconnects.

Will it change my existing printers?

It switches them to the CrispDock driver but keeps their names, so tools that send raw ZPL to them keep working. Uninstalling restores the built-in driver.

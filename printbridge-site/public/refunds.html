Refund Policy

Last updated: October 15, 2026

CrispDock is free to try — the free version is the full app with a watermark on printed labels — so you can make sure it works with your printer before you pay.

30-day refunds

If CrispDock isn’t right for you, email support@crispdock.com within 30 days of a payment with the email address you bought with, and we’ll refund that payment in full. This applies to monthly, yearly and lifetime payments, including renewals.

Refunds go back to the original payment method, usually within 5–10 business days depending on your bank.

What happens to the licence

Once a payment is refunded, the licence key it paid for stops working right away and CrispDock goes back to printing with the watermark. A refunded subscription is cancelled.

Cancelling a subscription

You can cancel at any time from your account page. Your licence keeps working until the end of the period you’ve paid for. Cancelling on its own doesn’t refund the current period — ask us within 30 days of the payment if you’d like that.

Switching to Lifetime

When you switch from a subscription to Lifetime, the unused days of your current period are taken off the Lifetime price. The subscription payments already made aren’t refunded separately.
